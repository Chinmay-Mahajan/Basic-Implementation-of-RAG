The Operator (The Ghost)
Role: The Antagonist / The Variable

Age/Status: Age indeterminate, operational asset for an elite, unindexed deep-state intelligence or corporate reconnaissance unit.

Visual Profile: Disguised by utility and normalcy. Wears a standard, dark, matte raincoat that blends into the monsoon night. Physically unhurried and disciplined. He carries no overt weapons—only the specialized hardware of his trade.

Personality & Psychology: Cold, efficient, and entirely devoid of malice or theatricality. He doesn't view Kabir as an enemy, but rather as an unexpected system bug that needs to be patched or reallocated. He respects efficiency, which is why he offers a dialogue at the end instead of immediate elimination. He operates with the absolute certainty of someone who commands the infrastructure around him.

Motivation: Maintenance of absolute operational invisibility. Protecting the "seams" of a massive, hidden surveillance and movement network.

Functional Role: He represents the terrifying intersection of physical force and high-tier technological dominance. He is the personification of the "system" fighting back against the individual.
